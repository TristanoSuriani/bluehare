# Blue Hare: identity

Blue Hare makes the FM-1 a performance instrument for bangers. It is a voice in a rig, sequenced from an external
sequencer (the Model:Cycles) or played by hand from a controller, and it sounds larger than life: aggressive, pure and
expressive leads and basses, and drums with weight. A few dramatic controls change the sound on the fly, and real sound
design happens on the device itself. The interface is tight and fast, and every screen pushes you towards making
music. It is not polite: the stock FM-1 firmware already covers that.

It is built for one person's setup first, because the best instruments are the ones you build for yourself.

## Rules

1. **Bangers first.** Every preset and every default is usable in a track at once: big, loud, finished.
2. **On the fly.** Each sound has a few performance knobs with dramatic range that cannot break it.
3. **Sound design on the device.** The plain pages are where a sound is designed, not just picked; EXPERT mode holds
   every parameter, out of the way.
4. **A tight interface.** Few pages, fast, no dead ends; everything leads back to playing.
5. **A good citizen in the rig.** Tight to external MIDI: mono from the sequencer, polyphonic from a controller, one
   patch per channel.
6. **A pianist's touch.** Velocity and dynamics must feel right.
7. **Safe to watch.** No flashing lights (or a safe mode that has none, if they ever come back).

## The rig

The Elektron Model:Cycles sequences and makes the main drums; the Roland S-1 and the FM-1 are voices played
monophonically from it, and polyphonically by hand from a MIDI controller. Blue Hare's drums are there to change things
up next to them, not to make it a groovebox.

## What follows from it (2026-10-18)

- Three tracks (2026-10-23, after the FM-1 itself), each with its own engines and its own MIDI input and channel
  (GLO > MIDI IN); no layer stacking.
- Engines, a track's own: 1 the synths VA SANDSTORM HOOVER SID OBIE (VA the virtual analogue; OBIE, SANDSTORM and
  HOOVER flavours of its core); 2 the kits KIT RHYTHM LINNKIT WAR (RHYTHM: the 808 and the 909, synthesized); 3 the
  voices SPEECH ONYX VOCODER VOSIM DUBSIREN. (2026-10-23: MODAL, BREAKS, DUBCHORD, DUBSIREN8 and WAVE archived: what
  sounds good on the device decides.)
- Sequenced from outside (2026-10-23): no sequencer of its own. REC records what the sequencer plays into each track's
  loop (SEQ > LOOP: BARS, QNT, CLEAR); a take replaces only the tracks that got notes. A performance instrument.
- The device's colours: the IDM palette (deep indigo, electric cyan, hot magenta), the studio's too.
- One global reverb (DRY SPRING GATED PLATE) and a delay per patch; chorus and EQ as presets, full controls in EXPERT.
- On ARP: the arpeggiator, a track's own, and its visuals while it plays (TECHNO archived 2026-10-23).
- The sound design studio follows the FM-1's structure, normal and EXPERT, with the same ARP and GLO.
